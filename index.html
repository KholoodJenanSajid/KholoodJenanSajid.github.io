02
Data Reconciliation Lead 2025 Amdocs · Deep-Dive
NEW

Device Sales cube reconciliation — separating loss from measurement.

Led end-to-end reconciliation of a production Device Sales analytical cube (Databricks gold vs legacy warehouse) whose grain is one current IMEI-sale after SCD-2, with retail / store / online branches and a 27-month date window.

Isolated cube COUNT (current dimension rows) from measures — SalePrice, discount, list/unit cost, retail price, OEM & channel subsidies, protect tier — that attach through a one-row-per-IMEI DeviceActivity rank. That way a matching order on a different device id can pass inventory checks and still fail dollars.

Classified residuals into SCD current-to-history flips, late IMEI assignment, and same-order IMEI twins. Proved a multi-day window had zero missing sales vs source (true_miss = 0) while documenting why IMEI-keyed sums and distinct-device counts still diverged.

Turned it into a repeatable certify pattern (business-date keys, not ETL stamps) that stakeholders now use to separate real loss from measure/join effects.
